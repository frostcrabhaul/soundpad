## How It Works

1. **Installation**: Begin by downloading Soundpad from the official source. Follow the installation prompts to set up the application on your Windows device. Once installed, launch the application to get started.

2. **Setup Soundboard**: Upon launching, you will see an empty soundboard interface. You can add your desired sound effects and music files by clicking the "Add Sound" button. You can organize these files into different categories or folders within the app for easy access. For instance, you might have a folder for gaming sounds, another for memes, and a third for music.

3. **Assign Hotkeys**: After adding sounds, customize your hotkeys for each sound effect. Click on the sound you want to assign a hotkey to and press the desired key combination. This allows for quick triggering of sounds while using other applications, without needing to switch back to Soundpad. Hotkeys can be set to combinations like Ctrl + Alt + [key], ensuring they do not interfere with common shortcuts in other applications.

4. **Use in Applications**: Open your preferred voice chat or gaming application. Ensure Soundpad is running in the background, and use the assigned hotkeys to play sounds during your conversations or gameplay. Ensure that in your voice chat application, the microphone input is set to "Stereo Mix" or "What U Hear" so that the sound from Soundpad is transmitted.

5. **Engage with Others**: As you interact with other users, feel free to use the sounds you’ve set up. Whether it’s a funny meme sound effect during a light-hearted conversation or a dramatic audio clip during a gaming session, Soundpad allows you to engage with your audience in a fun and interactive way.
